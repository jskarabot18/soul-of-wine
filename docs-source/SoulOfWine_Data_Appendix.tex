\section*{D.3 Terroir Cluster Membership}
The terroir partition is fully model-derived: Layer 2 TF-IDF $\rightarrow$ PCA (10 components) $\rightarrow$ K-means ($k = 7$). Cluster count, membership, and naming are determined by the algorithm with no manual override. Top TF-IDF terms indicate the vocabulary features that most distinguish each cluster.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=2em,itemsep=0.4em]
\item[Iberian Continental (6).] Top terms: \emph{spain, rioja, do.}\\
Douro, Catalonia, Galicia, Ribera del Duero, Rioja, Mendoza.

\item[Southern Hemisphere \& International (12).] Top terms: \emph{zealand, new zealand, australia.}\\
Alto Adige, Patagonia, Barossa Valley, Hunter Valley, Margaret River, Maipo Valley, Central Otago, Hawke's Bay, Marlborough, Stellenbosch, Swartland, Napa Valley.

\item[American West Coast (7).] Top terms: \emph{ava, wineries, california.}\\
Columbia Valley, Paso Robles, Santa Barbara, Santa Cruz Mountains, Sonoma, Walla Walla, Willamette Valley.

\item[French Viticultural (10).] Top terms: \emph{france, c\^ote, de.}\\
Alsace, Beaujolais, Bordeaux, Burgundy, Champagne, Ch\^ateauneuf-du-Pape, Jura, Loire, Northern Rh\^one, Provence.

\item[Germanic Rhine (5).] Top terms: \emph{germany, vdp, mosel.}\\
Baden, Mosel, Nahe, Pfalz, Rheingau.

\item[Austrian Danube (3).] Top terms: \emph{dac, veltliner, austria.}\\
Kamptal, Wachau, Wagram.

\item[Mediterranean \& Volcanic (16).] Top terms: \emph{italy, di, indigenous.}\\
Steiermark, Dalmatian Coast, Macedonia, Santorini, Tokaj, Campania, Etna, Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Liguria, Piedmont, Sardinia, Sicily, Tuscany, Veneto, Gori\v{s}ka Brda, Finger Lakes.
\end{description}

\section*{D.6 SME Change Log}
Sixteen score and metaphor changes across thirteen regions, applied after subject-matter expert review of the Pass 5 D-score matrix. All changes are final and incorporated into the canonical documents. Three intentional cluster shifts resulted: Etna and Santa Cruz Mountains $\rightarrow$ Old World Interior; Nahe and Wachau $\rightarrow$ The Moderates; Sonoma $\rightarrow$ New World Reinvention.

Champagne        & Society       & $D_3$: $+1 \to +2$ & Matches Bordeaux reasoning; tradition as dominant institutional force. \\
Etna             & Awakening     & $D_1$: $-1 \to +1$, $D_2$: $+1 \to +2$, $D_3$: $-1 \to 0$ & Volcanic introspection is interior; extreme conditions justify $+2$ struggle. Cluster shift: $\to$ Old World Interior. \\
